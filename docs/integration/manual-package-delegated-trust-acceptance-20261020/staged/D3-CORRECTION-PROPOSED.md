## Exact proposed replacement

For a first Browser, the following is a PROPOSED explicit independent administrative handover ceremony, requiring owner approval before acceptance. It is not an implemented source or a change to M0-M8. The Browser operator must already recognize the administrator's identity and administrative role through a trusted relationship independent of this package, its delivery, its password and the candidate server connection. The minimal proposed channel is an in-person handover by that independently recognized administrator. If that pre-existing recognition or role cannot be established, deny; do not infer it from a displayed name, certificate, operating-system group or a successful connection. No alternative channel is silently selected.

The administrator must consult the actual D1 approval through the existing root/policy owner at the applicable factual CURRENT revision. The handover must identify the exact full SHA256 fingerprint of complete signer DER SPKI (all32 bytes, not a shortened code), signer/issuer generation and package purpose, intended server/root scope, validity and certificate/delegation status at that administrative observation. The producer must validate the signer certificate and exact delegation under D2 before making that assertion. Issuance alone, codeSigning alone or a CA inside the package cannot provide that approval. The source and observation revision must be explicit; no live or globally fresh revocation fact is inferred from an offline signature.

NEW TRUST DECISION PROPOSED: for the first-contact handover, the independently recognized administrator's explicit attestation of the D2 purpose/delegation/status facts may constitute the approved signer context, together with exact fingerprint comparison. The Browser must distinguish that administrative attestation from local cryptographic validation of a signer certificate it has not received. This is the additional human trust assumption requiring explicit acceptance; an unverified user checkbox or an assertion extracted from the package is not that attestation. If the deployment requires independent public-certificate evidence beyond this attestation, its approved existing delivery/reference must be available; absent required evidence, deny. No new material-file format, mandatory second file, trust service or PapinhoTrustStore is introduced.

Before KDF, verify the independent package signature and compare the complete SPKI fingerprint and applicable scope against this independent approved context. Preserve immutable input/object identity across all stages. The package may supply untrusted bytes for mathematical verification but cannot supply its own trust decision. Server/root values inside ciphertext are checked against the independently approved scope only after AEAD; do not read unauthenticated plaintext to establish scope or trust.

The handover is specific to the present import/confirmation operation and is not a reusable certificate-status freshness grant. Require an explicit applicable status policy before use: the proposed minimum is a contemporaneous administrative observation tied to that same handover/import operation, without automatic caching/reuse. This does not establish global latest-state/fencing or immunity to revocation after observation, and does not invent a numerical TTL or network dependency. If the applicable status evidence or policy is insufficient, uncertain or unavailable, deny before KDF. An interrupted/replaced operation requires a newly validated independent context, not automatic reuse of prior confirmation.

Signature verification, signer trust and local installation permission are three distinct outcomes. After complete authenticated content/certificate/key/identity validation, an operator already permitted by the existing BrowserProfile policy to change that profile must explicitly authorize installation of the identified configuration. The remote administrator's attestation, password entry or a trusted signer does not grant that local permission. Validation may succeed without installation; cancel preserves the old committed configuration. This local authorization is not an Accelerator Principal, grant, admission or authority to modify policy beyond existing rights.

The existing candidate/generation/reference/store/lease owner must retain the independent confirmation facts and references through validation and any authorized commit, using its existing lifecycle only. Required ownership, storage representation and audit without secrets must be qualified before implementation. No raw string/boolean, decoded record or new registry may substitute for this provenance. Producer CURRENT, independently established Browser trust, and Browser installation authorization remain separate. All absent/ambiguous/revoked/expired/mismatched/Stop/uncertain cases deny; no first-use auto-trust, silent persistence, fallback, new codec or operational effect follows from this proposal.
