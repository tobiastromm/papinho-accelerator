## Problem and proposed decision

The [supported-interface feasibility review](../integration/bd-r2-native-console-proof-feasibility-20261024/closeout.md) concluded NOT_DEMONSTRATED, not universal impossibility. Authentication of the complete console-endpoint/original-host/mapped-image tuple is unqualified. Console transport is not the administrative principal. It is technically reasonable to consider a narrower auxiliary-resource classification, but changing that boundary is a SECURITY/CLASSIFICATION decision, not an ED11 kernel-cost uncertainty exception or an editorial clarification.

Recommend A for bounded review: identify exactly one live auxiliary candidate using the supported observations below, keep the console as interactive transport, and charge it fully. Do not assert authenticated host ownership or mapped-image provenance. Approval would explicitly change the evidence demanded of this non-authoritative resource while preserving all mandatory actor/qualifier/service authority checks. If that classification assurance is insufficient to preserve rejection of arbitrary/unrelated processes, reject A and retain D; no implementation may paper over that deficiency.

## Proposed replacement R6 — authority, transport loss and Stop

R6.N1. Before admission/effects require the original authorized operator's usable interactive console, exact plan/attempt confirmation and continuous elevated/non-impersonating actor binding. The console is a transport/input dependency; its host is neither credential nor Stop owner. Elevation alone never substitutes confirmation. The existing canonical native owner alone validates/consumes authorization and governs effect, Stop, retention and finish.

R6.N2. Distinguish CONSOLE_USABLE, CONFIRMATION_ACCEPTED, OPERATION_AUTHORIZED, STOP_REQUESTED, STOP_ACCEPTED, QUIESCENCE_PROVED, STOPPED_FACTUAL and REFERENCES_CLOSED. None implies the next. A console control notification may request cancellation/Stop through the owner's existing bounded path; it does not attest the sender as administrative authority, authorize Start/rebind or prove completed Stop. Cancellation must not enlarge authority. No new owner, service, registry, worker or recorder.

R6.N3. Console/actor loss or Stop before effects suppresses effects and follows factual close/retention. Loss after effect suppresses further effects and latches failure; request only the already-authorized single safe Stop through the live canonical owner WHEN legal and possible. Do not synthesize an SCM Stop, extra retry, kill or authority after owner death. Capture STOP_REQUESTED separately from service acceptance, runtime quiescence, SCM STOPPED/process-exit facts and reference closure. Preserve first failure. A missing notification or abrupt process termination may leave UNKNOWN; no guarantee that every console close permits cleanup is claimed.

R6.N4. No detached/unattended operation is authorized by accepting this clarification. No automatic AttachConsole/FreeConsole repair, rebinding, replacement operator, automatic rearm or continuation after control loss. Existing pins/receipts/charges/held references remain governed by Model E until factual release or separately authorized historical disposition. Process/window disappearance, elapsed timeout and closed ETL/console never substitute Stop/quiescence. The owner need not terminate the OS console host to close its own original references, nor claim that doing so frees all kernel resources. A genuinely console-independent handoff/Stop path is alternative B/C and needs its own normative and implementation qualification before use.
