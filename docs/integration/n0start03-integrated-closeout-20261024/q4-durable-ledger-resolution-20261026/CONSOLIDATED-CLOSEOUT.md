## Recommended architecture and decision
A specific PROPOSED ADR contains L0-L7. Preserve the required rollback guarantee by reusing the existing root as LOCAL substrate and requiring a factual independently retained consumption witness outside the entire local restoration domain. Before adapter implementation, identify an existing governed witness with authenticated identity, exclusive compare/consume, durable spent query, non-rollback/fork assumptions, failure semantics, custody and resource budget. No such mechanism or identity is invented. If absent, authorizing one narrow witness boundary is a real new architectural decision. No new permanent service is implied or installed.

Consumption must bind the new request, historic operation, current context, exact original reader PID/creation and artifacts/objects. Only the original live consuming invocation may continue once; a restarted invocation querying matching CONSUMED records still denies. Local/witness disagreement, missing records, uncertain consumption/flush/close, substituted identity or failed witness query denies and retains first failure/dependencies. Query-only reconciliation never refunds or reissues a spent approval. Merely finding two matching records is not admission.

Alternative B is to keep the accepted contract and leave readback denied. A local-only fault-model exception resembling M3 would reduce full rollback protection, is NOT recommended, and would require separate explicit approval. The present task requests no weakening, so no local marker is promoted as the solution.
