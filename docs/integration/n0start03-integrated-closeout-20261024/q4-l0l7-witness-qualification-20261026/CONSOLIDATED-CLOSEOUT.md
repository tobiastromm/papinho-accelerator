## Witness options and guarantee boundaries
TPM2 NV counter is now a real locally available candidate. To become independent witness it needs a dedicated approved index/public policy/Name and proven same-device generation outside the ledger rollback domain. Manufacturer strings/interface and GetCapability cannot distinguish every cloned/virtualized/firmware scenario or attest device identity. Hardware-backed key storage/sealing/PCRs alone do not count one-use authorizations. Clear, deletion/redefinition, hardware replacement, vTPM snapshot, power-loss/orderly writeback and authorization loss are unresolved lifecycle/threat cases; all must deny rather than reset baseline. NV increment is not application CAS, so two-store sequencing and exact expected value/race handling remain mandatory.

Existing externally governed authority was not identified in reviewed project mechanisms. No remote account/service discovery or network contact was performed. Existing owner decision history is not an authenticated atomic durable consumption endpoint by itself. A second same-domain disk/registry/hash chain cannot solve coherent rollback. No infrastructure is installed just to bypass this finding.
