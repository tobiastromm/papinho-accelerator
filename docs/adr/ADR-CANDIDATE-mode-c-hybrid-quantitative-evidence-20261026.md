# Candidate — hybrid quantitative evidence with explicit unqualified categories

This candidate supplements the incomplete alternatives in ADR-CANDIDATE-mode-c-target-evidence-policy-20261026.md. Neither candidate is ACCEPTED. The owner's hybrid investigation direction is not normative approval, empirical residual acceptance, an operational scope or capacity grant. Existing accepted Mode C evidence-validity semantics remain controlling. R/H/Q/T LAB decisions do not transfer to this product target.

## Proposed rule HQT1 — separate guarantees and budgets

A hard-controlled category requires an exact source/compiler/artifact bound with unit, lifetime, multiplicity, owning scope and non-overlap proof. A process counter or empirical maximum is never that bound. An opaque category may use delimited empirical coverage only after an exact supported envelope and explicit owner decision name the guarantee being relaxed. If either required layer is absent, RESOURCE_UNKNOWN and DENY persist. Shared runtime/storage is charged once in its actual owner; retained charges stay unavailable. Different process budgets are not fungible.

## HQT2 — verified numeric ingredients, not grants

For current compiled source profiles: Accelerator C2 manager527,824 bytes (two embedded operations); C3 bridge132,448 bytes (two embedded bridges in C8 storage). Controlled payload subset393,216 bytes per operation is included in those values, not additional. Existing known-managed totals are1,965,128/3,145,728 bytes Selected and1,799,632/2,097,152 Legacy. They cover the macro's declared fixed scope only, not the entire provider/kernel target.

VC6 Browser's exact existing reservation expression is1,858,020 bytes per ordinary child operation against2,097,152 controlled bytes. Two charges total3,716,040, therefore the present implementation cannot promise two simultaneous ordinary reservations. The maximum of two slots still includes retained charges. No cap increase, transfer of charges, fictitious reuse or altered expression follows. Even one reservation remains denied until other required authorities/evidence exist.

Measured runtime-core samples cover20 fresh processes/240 lifetimes/500 samples with public providers initialized0. Zero sampled per-process private/handle deltas are observations of an uninitialized-provider core, not zero allocation, active TLS cost, hard ceiling, memory headroom, leak proof or refund. Absolute values and backend/configuration/lifetime bindings are recorded in the evidence package.

## HQT3 — categories and evidence requirements

Each annex entry must carry version, category/unit, original process/object identity, owner, origin API, begin/completion observation times in its own clock domain, lifetime, multiplicity, artifact/backend/profile/recipe identity, validity/expiry/invalidation, knownness and proof reference. Existing typed producers are reused; this candidate creates no wire/API or new service/authority.

Controlled embedded buffers and existing reservation expressions have compiler-backed bounds. Active initialization/TLS/session/cache/thread/allocator costs, socket/global/shared kernel costs and unsampled transients remain UNKNOWN. Process private/commit/working set and paged/nonpaged quota counters overlap and do not partition all resources. No sum of these overlapping metrics is allowed as a fabricated total. Source-package hashes do not attest a runtime mapped module.

## HQT4 — empirical acceptance boundary

Before selecting empirical coverage, the owner must receive an exact one-operation correlated Browser/C2/C3/provider recipe and representative success, failure, retained and Stop evidence. State workloads/backends/builds/modules/host/topology supported and excluded; observation overhead; sampling blind spots; unsupported failure modes; invalidation on change; and why a universal provider/kernel bound cannot be supplied. Sample maxima are data, not ceilings or probability guarantees. No arbitrary multiplier, per-connection constant, confidence claim or margin is selected here.

The missing target freshness values, host physical/commit conditions and opaque coverage limits remain PENDING_EVIDENCE_AND_DECISION. LAB60s/1s,5/9GiB and raw C8 diagnostic1000ms are not inherited. Applicable native/admin limits remain128/1024 in their existing LAB scope; no grant or quota change is introduced. The existing Browser2MiB/Accelerator2MiB-or-selected3MiB managed caps remain unchanged.

## HQT5 — admission, Stop and retention

Required unknown/missing/stale/replayed/mismatched evidence, controlled lifetime uncertainty, incompatible reservation or failed effective identity/access check => DENY. Preserve first error, no acquisition after freeze, exactly-once, governed Stop when legal, dependency retention and factual close. No rollback/retry/refund from disappearance or from public void release. Empirical residual acceptance cannot waive ownership or falsify controlled closure.

## HQT6 — next decision and alternatives

A. Supply complete hard envelopes for all mandatory active categories; no empirical exception.
B. Authorize a narrowly defined independent diagnostic to collect active composition evidence, then decide an exact delimited empirical policy annex. This is a future decision, not automatic selection of B. Current admission denial cannot be bypassed to collect its own missing prerequisite.
C. Keep target disabled and UNKNOWN until sufficient evidence/authority exists. This remains actual behavior.

No executable operational request or owner approval is created by this ADR. Q4 readback/disposition and future service readiness/Stop require independent scopes; no consumed identity is reused. E19/E20/E22 and ED11/Phase6 remain PARTIAL. CURRENT/signature/authoritative recovery remain DENY.
